## English

**BestSchool** is a free, ad-free learning app that lets 1st graders practise maths with numbers from 0 to 20 in a playful way.
It runs entirely in the browser – no installation, no sign-up, no internet connection and no data collection.
It is available in **English and German** (toggle **EN/DE** in the top right corner).

### ✨ Topics

| Topic | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 (Expert) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🐸 **Counting** | what comes next (to 10), complete a sequence, frog hops | to 20, forwards **and** backwards | several gaps, long jumps |
| 🐊 **Bigger · smaller** | compare amounts with dots (ten-frame) | to 20, “greater/less than …” | signs `<` `=` `>` with the crocodile trick |
| 🐝 **In between** | number between A and B on a number line (to 10) | to 20, boundary numbers as traps | tap **all** numbers between A and B |

There is also a **🎲 Mixed bag** with tasks from every topic.

### 🧠 Teaching approach

- **Everything is read aloud** – children do not need to read fluently yet (🔊 to repeat).
- **Visual models:** number line, ten-frame with groups of five, and number sequences as a bridge to abstract numbers.
- **Learning from mistakes:** a hint after the first mistake, an explained solution after the second (e.g. the frog hops to the answer). No penalty points.
- **Levels:** 6 out of 8 right on the first try unlocks the next level.
- **Motivation:** a star ⭐ for every task solved first time, a new sticker for every 5 stars.
- **Short rounds** of 8 tasks – ideal for 5–10 minutes of focused practice.

### 🚀 Getting started

**Download:** click **Code → Download ZIP**, unzip it and double-click `index.html`.

**Online via GitHub Pages:** in the repository go to **Settings → Pages** and choose branch `main` and folder `/ (root)`.
The app will then be available at `https://<username>.github.io/<repository>/`.
Add `?lang=en` or `?lang=de` to the address to open it in that language.

Recommended browsers: current versions of Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.

**🗣️ Reading voice:** the app automatically picks the most natural female voice available on the device. Parents can also choose and preview a voice in the parents' area.
The free **“Natural” voices in Microsoft Edge** (e.g. Sonia or Libby, internet required) sound best, followed by the Google voices in Chrome.
